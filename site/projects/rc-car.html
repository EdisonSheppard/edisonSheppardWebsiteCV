MECHANICAL DESIGN / RC ENGINEERING

Custom Two-Speed
RC Car.

A remote-controlled car built around mechanical experimentation: gearing, steering, articulated suspension and a chassis designed to bring it all together.

Construction / 3D-printed mechanismsDrivetrain / Two-speed designFocus / Suspension & gearing
01 / OVERVIEW

A mechanical experiment on wheels.

The build combines a fabricated chassis, 3D-printed suspension components, a geared drive system, steering linkages and exposed control electronics. Rather than concealing the mechanisms, the open layout makes the design and assembly visible.

The project explores how drive ratios, steering geometry and independent suspension can fit into a small vehicle. The accompanying photographs show the build from multiple viewpoints, including the suspension and exposed drivetrain.

Detailed development notes, design calculations and test results will be added when the source documentation is supplied.
